Privacy note

Your words, handled with care.

The short version: we only collect what we need, nothing is published unless we choose it, and you can ask us to remove your answer at any time.

What we collect

  • Your answer to the week's prompt.
  • The name you choose to show, which can be a pseudonym. Or nothing, if you choose Anonymous.
  • Your email. It's needed if you share under a name, and optional if you choose Anonymous. It is never shown on the site.
  • Contact messages you send us: your first name, email, subject and message.

How we use it

  • Answers are read before anything is shared. The answers we choose may be published and shared in any to truly live project, online or in print (like our website, social media, emails, or a future book or magazine), always under the name you picked.
  • We use your email only to reach you about your answer (for example, to ask before featuring it) or to reply to your message. We don't sell it or share it.
  • We only add you to our weekly email if you tick "Also send me the weekly prompt" or subscribe yourself. We keep a record of when you said yes, and you can unsubscribe anytime from the bottom of any email.

Where it's stored

Your information is stored securely with the service providers that run this site (Vercel and Upstash, for hosting and storage) and Substack (for our email list). These providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of those countries.

Removing your answer

Changed your mind? Send us a note through Contact us with the week and the name you used, and we'll take it down from anywhere we can. We can't recall anything that's already been printed.
