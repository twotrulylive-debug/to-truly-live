# To Truly Live

**A space for women to reconnect with themselves, explore what’s possible, and build lives that feel like their own.**

To Truly Live is a women’s wellness and lifestyle platform built around the idea that living well means more than simply getting through the day. It means having the space, support, and freedom to discover who you are, what matters to you, and what it looks like to live fully on your own terms.

Created to bring together thoughtful storytelling, creative expression, wellness resources, and a community of women doing meaningful work, To Truly Live is a space for women to feel seen, inspired, and empowered to explore a different way of living.

## Our Vision

We believe women deserve more than a life built around expectations. They deserve lives rooted in intention, connection, creativity, and well-being.

To Truly Live exists to make space for honest conversations, diverse experiences, and the people and practices that help women feel more connected to themselves and their communities.

## What We're Building

- **Stories & Perspectives:** A growing collection of personal stories, reflections, art, and creative work that explore what it means to truly live.
- **The Collective:** A space to discover inspiring women, independent businesses, wellness practitioners, and people contributing to a more thoughtful approach to living.
- **Wellness & Discovery:** Resources and perspectives that encourage women to explore their well-being, personal growth, and everyday choices.
- **Community & Connection:** Opportunities to connect women with ideas, people, and resources that support the lives they want to create.

## The Website

The To Truly Live website is the foundation of this vision. It brings together editorial content, profiles, and discovery features in one place, with room to grow into a broader platform for community engagement and sustainable membership offerings.

**Explore the website:** [To Truly Live](https://to-truly-live.vercel.app/index.html)

## Our Approach

To Truly Live is being built with intention: prioritizing meaningful content, thoughtful design, accessibility, and genuine connection over noise and trends.

The long-term vision is to grow beyond a digital publication into a sustainable platform that supports women, celebrates their work, and makes meaningful wellness resources easier to discover.

## Development

This repository contains the website for To Truly Live. Development is ongoing as the platform's content, features, and visual identity continue to evolve.

Future development may include:
- Expanded editorial and storytelling features
- Individual profiles and collective discovery tools
- Newsletter and community features
- Memberships and recurring subscription offerings
- Additional wellness resources and partnerships

## The Bigger Picture

To Truly Live is more than a website. It is an evolving vision for a world where women have more opportunities to feel supported, explore their potential, and define success and well-being for themselves.

**Not just existing. Truly living.**
